Insight Lens beta: what 11,000 comments showed us

Four teams, one month and a pile of comments that fit no theme.

IN✦
PRPriya Raman
Data Office

Insight Lens went into beta with four teams in September: Customer Services, People & Culture, Communications and Policy & Planning. Between them they uploaded about 11,000 free-text comments from surveys and feedback forms. These are our notes from the first month.

Theme quality was good enough to edit

Our bar for the beta was that analysts would rather edit the suggested themes than start from scratch. All four teams cleared it. On the quarterly service survey, the tool proposed 23 themes. The analysts merged six pairs, split one theme in two, renamed most of them and ended with 18. That took about three hours, compared with the four days the manual tagging usually needs.

We checked accuracy by having an analyst hand-tag a random 300 comments. The tool's theme matched the analyst's for 79% of them. Most mismatches were comments that fairly belonged to two themes.

The outlier pile was the surprise

Each run puts comments that fit no theme into a separate list. We expected it to be noise. In Customer Services' data it held 40 comments about a new online form rejecting certain postcodes. That issue had not reached the call statistics yet because people were giving up instead of calling. The form was fixed within a week.

We would never have found that by counting categories. It was 40 comments out of 4,000.

What needs work

  • Small groups. People & Culture raised a concern that themes with only two or three comments could identify staff in small teams. Themes under five comments are now hidden by default.
  • Long comments. Comments over 300 words were often split across themes in ways that lost their meaning. We are testing a summary step first.
  • Trends. Teams want to compare survey waves. A first version of the comparison view ships next month.

Joining the beta

We are adding three more teams in the next round. If your team runs a survey with free-text questions at least twice a year, contact the Insight Lens squad through the product page.
