Why we retired Summary Bot

Sunsetting a tool 300 people used every week, and what we learned about telling them.

WH✦
GTGrace Teo
Communications

Summary Bot was one of our first tools. You pasted a long email thread and got back five lines: what was being asked, what had been decided, and who was waiting on whom. At its peak about 300 people used it every week, and its satisfaction scores were the highest of any tool we ran.

We retired it in August. This post explains why, and how we handled it.

The overlap problem

By early this year, Doc Companion could summarise any pasted text, and Meeting Scribe handled transcripts. When we looked at a sample of 200 Summary Bot requests, 170 were jobs Doc Companion now did as well or better. The remaining 30 were mostly meeting transcripts, which Meeting Scribe handled better.

Running three tools with overlapping jobs cost us more than server time. Every model upgrade had to be tested three times. Every policy change meant three sets of instructions. Our release cycle had slowed from two weeks to five.

How we decided

We listed every job people used Summary Bot for, matched each one to a replacement, and timed the replacement on real examples. Only when every job had a replacement that was at least as fast did we set a date.

How we told people

  • Six weeks' notice, in the tool itself and by email to regular users
  • A side-by-side guide: "If you used Summary Bot for X, use Y"
  • Two drop-in sessions to try the replacements with your own examples
  • A banner on the last day linking to the guide

We had 14 complaints, mostly about losing the short five-line format. We added a "five-line summary" option to Doc Companion in response, and most of those users moved over within a fortnight.

What we would do again

Timing the replacements on real examples before announcing anything meant we could answer "is the new tool as good?" with numbers. Weekly active use of Doc Companion rose by 210 people in the month after the switch, which accounts for most of Summary Bot's former users.
