What you can and cannot paste into an AI tool

A plain-language guide to data handling, with the five questions to ask before you paste anything.

WH✦
RKRavi Kumar
Legal

The question we hear most at office hours is some version of "Am I allowed to paste this?" The short answer depends on two things: which tool you are using, and what is in the text. This post covers both.

Which tools are approved

The tools listed on this hub run on models hosted inside our own environment. Text you type is logged for 30 days for quality review, then deleted, and it is never used to train a model. Public chatbots on the open internet do not meet these conditions. Do not paste work content into them, even if it seems harmless.

What counts as personal data

Names, ID numbers, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses are obvious. Less obvious are combinations: "the 54-year-old officer in the Northgate branch who filed a complaint last week" can identify one person without a name. Case notes almost always contain these combinations.

Five questions before you paste

  1. Is this an approved tool from the hub's product list?
  2. Does the text contain names, ID numbers or contact details?
  3. Could someone work out who this is from the details left in?
  4. Is any of it classified above our standard internal level?
  5. Would you be comfortable if the content owner saw you paste it?

If the answer to question 1 is no, stop. If any of questions 2 to 4 is yes, remove or replace the details first.

Placeholders work well. Replace names with [Customer], [Officer] or [Branch]. The model writes around them, and you fill the real details in afterwards.

Special cases

Insight Lens is approved for survey comments, including free text that may contain personal details, because the data stays in a restricted workspace and themes with fewer than five comments are hidden. Query Desk only connects to datasets the Data Office has already de-identified. Neither of these makes it acceptable to paste the same content into a different tool.

If you make a mistake

It happens. If you paste personal data into a tool by accident, tell the Data Office the same day. Logs can be deleted early on request, and reporting it quickly is always treated as the right thing to do.
