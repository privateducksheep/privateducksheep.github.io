The AI champions network after one year

Thirty-one volunteers across eight teams, what they do each month, and what we would change.

TH✦
SLShaun Lim
Digital Products

A year ago we asked each team to nominate one or two people who were already using AI tools and were happy to help colleagues start. Thirty-one people signed up across eight teams. None of them are AI specialists, and the network works partly because of that.

What champions do

  • Run a 15-minute demo at their team meeting, once a quarter
  • Collect questions and bring them to office hours
  • Share one real use case a month in the champions channel
  • Tell the AI team when something is confusing or broken

We ask for about two hours a month. In return, champions get early access to new features, a monthly call with the product teams, and their name on the use cases they contribute.

What the numbers show

Teams with an active champion have roughly twice the weekly active users of teams without one: 41% of staff compared with 19%. Some of that is selection, since keen teams nominate keen champions. But the two teams that gained a champion mid-year both doubled their usage within three months, which suggests the champion makes a real difference.

Champions also give us better feedback. About half of the bugs reported last quarter came from them, and their reports usually include the exact steps and a screenshot.

What did not work

We started with a monthly all-champions meeting. Attendance dropped from 26 to 9 by month four. People had nothing to report on a fixed schedule. We replaced it with a short written update and a call only when a product team has something to show.

We also underestimated turnover. Seven champions moved roles during the year, and in three cases nobody replaced them. We now ask every champion to name a backup.

Next year

We want every team to have at least two champions, and we are writing a starter kit so new champions do not have to learn everything from the channel history. If you want to join, talk to your team lead or reply to this post.
