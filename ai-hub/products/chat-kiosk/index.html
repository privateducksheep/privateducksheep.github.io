Data handling

Runs on approved, internally hosted models. What you type is logged for 30 days for quality review and is never used to train models.

What it does

Our first visitor-facing pilot. It answered common questions at two service centres for eight months. Usage was steady, but most questions were already covered by signage and the queue system, so its answer engine moved into Answer Studio.

Why we built it

Service centres wanted to shorten queues for simple questions such as opening hours and document checklists.

Visitors liked it, but they mostly asked what the signs already said.

Key features

  • Touchscreen question flow
  • Queue number lookup
  • Handover to a staff member
